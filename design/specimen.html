Design specimen

Fresh, calm and grown-up: cream for space, black type, and colour used as whole cards with black text on top. Blue leads; yellow, pink and green each get one card at most per screen.

Logo

Logo in colour

The colours are light, so a coloured logo only goes on black. On a coloured ground, the logo is black. On cream the full logo stays black, because the thin letters fade in colour; the icon alone can be coloured (see below).

Icon on its own

For tight spaces: the app header, browser tab, avatar fallbacks and loading states. Same rule as the logo: colour on black, black on colour.

Colour on cream

Flat colour on cream, from 48 px up: welcome screens, empty states, loading, section markers. Blue first; yellow, pink and green for small accents. Below 48 px, use the black icon so it stays readable.
