## 01 · Intelly, healthcare app

- Link: https://www.behance.net/gallery/191973409/Intelly-Healthcare-App-UI-Branding
- Image: `01-intelly-dashboard.webp` (local only)
- Use it for: **how colour is used**. Not for layout or components.

### What it does well with colour

1. **Colour is a surface, never text.** Pastels fill whole cards; every word, number and chart mark on them is black.
2. **Charts are black on colour.** No multi-coloured charts; the card colour is the only colour.
3. **Tone-on-tone shapes.** A large shape in a slightly deeper shade of the card's own colour adds depth without adding a colour.
4. **Black anchors the pastels.** Black sidebar, black pill buttons, black active filter. It keeps the soft colours from looking sweet.
5. **Cream ground, quiet lists.** Lists sit on cream or near-white; colour only marks the selected row and small category icons.
6. **Each colour means one thing.** A colour is tied to a category and stays tied to it.

### What we do differently

- **Blue leads.** Intelly gives yellow, pink, green and blue equal space, which reads playful. In Apiona, blue is the main coloured surface; yellow, pink and green appear at most once per screen, as a marker or one small card.
- **Selection is blue or black, not pink.**
- **Our colours already carry meaning in the record:** blue for the patient's own words, yellow for flags, pink for dismissals, green for confirmed findings. Category colours must not clash with those.
- **The tone-on-tone shape is our icon.** Where a card needs a shape, it's the Apiona mark in a deeper shade of the card colour.
